## Phase 2: Cold Call Scripts

### Opening (first 10 seconds — make or break)

**Version A: The straight shooter**
> "Hi [name], it's [your name] from Acumei. I help [industry] businesses automate the admin that eats their evenings — things like [one specific pain]. Got 30 seconds?"

**Version B: The email follow-up**
> "Hi [name], it's [your name] — I sent you a quick email a couple of days ago about [topic]. Just wanted to see if it landed and if it's worth a quick chat?"

**Version C: The referral angle**
> "Hi [name], I've been working with a few [industry] businesses in [area] and your name kept coming up. We help with AI automation — got a minute?"

### If they say "What do you do?"

> "In a nutshell — we build AI assistants for businesses like yours. So instead of you or your team spending hours on [specific task], it just happens automatically. We built one for a [similar business] in [city] and it [specific result]. The whole thing took about two weeks."

### If they say "We're not interested"

> "Totally fair. Out of curiosity, is it because you've already got something in place, or is it more of a timing thing? Just so I know whether to check back in or leave you be."

### If they say "Send me something"

> "Happy to. What's the best email? I'll send over a quick one-pager with a couple of examples from [their industry]. And if anything catches your eye, we can jump on a 15-minute call — no pressure."

### If they say "How much does it cost?"

> "Depends on what we build, but most SME projects land between £2K and £10K as a one-off. We quote you properly on the discovery call once we understand what you need. The call itself is free and there's zero commitment."

### If they're interested — book the call

> "Brilliant. What works better for you — mornings or afternoons? I've got [slot 1] and [slot 2] this week. It's 30 minutes, I'll look at your setup beforehand and come with some ideas. I'll send a calendar invite to [email] — sound good?"

### Voicemail script (leave if they don't pick up)

> "Hi [name], quick message — it's [your name] from Acumei. We help [industry] businesses automate the repetitive stuff that eats your time. I'll drop you a short email so you've got my details. No rush, but if you're curious, I'd love a quick chat. Cheers."

## Phase 4: Discovery Call Structure (30 min)

### Minute 0-5: Build rapport
- "Tell me about [business name] — how long have you been running it?"
- "What does a typical day look like for you?"

### Minute 5-15: Find the pain
- "What takes up the most time that you wish you didn't have to do?"
- "What happens when you miss a call / forget to follow up / run out of stock?"
- "How much would you say that costs you — in time or money?"
- "Have you tried fixing it before? What happened?"

### Minute 15-22: Show the solution
- "Here's what we'd build for you..." (walk through a specific scenario from their industry)
- "It would plug into [their existing tools] — you wouldn't need to change anything"
- "You'd own the whole thing — code, prompts, everything"

### Minute 22-27: Handle objections
- **"Too expensive"** → "Let's look at the calculator — if it saves X hours/week at £Y/hour, it pays for itself in Z weeks"
- **"Not sure about AI"** → "That's exactly why we do the discovery call first. If AI isn't the right fit, I'll tell you"
- **"Need to think about it"** → "Completely understand. What would help you decide? Happy to send a written brief"

### Minute 27-30: Close or next step
- "Based on what we've talked about, I think [specific solution] would work well. Want me to put together a brief?"
- "I can have a written proposal with scope and pricing to you by [day]. Sound good?"
