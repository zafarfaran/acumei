# Module 3: Service & Pricing Model

## Executive Summary

- **Recommended pricing positions the "AI Brain" offering in the gap between freelancers ($75-200/hr) and boutique firms ($200-400/hr), targeting blended effective rates of $175-325/hr depending on segment** — high enough to signal expertise, low enough to undercut firms with overhead.
- **Gross margins range from 70-85% on solo-delivered services (Diagnostic, Strategy) and 50-65% on contractor-augmented Builds** — making the business highly viable even at modest utilization, with retainer support as the highest-margin recurring revenue stream at 75-85%.
- **A single mid-market client progressing through the full service ladder (Free Scan → Diagnostic → Build → Support) generates $73,000-$133,000 in 12-month LTV**, making 3-5 active client relationships sufficient to sustain a profitable solo practice.

## Service Ladder

### Tier 0: Free AI Opportunity Scan (Lead Generation)

**What it is:** A complimentary 45-60 minute discovery call with a brief written deliverable (1-2 pages) identifying the client's top 3 AI opportunities ranked by impact and feasibility.

**Deliverables:**
- Pre-call: 15-minute review of company website, product, and public information
- Live call: 45-60 minute structured conversation mapping current operations, pain points, and data assets
- Post-call: 1-2 page "AI Opportunity Brief" — top 3 opportunities with rough sizing (impact, complexity, timeline)

**Timeline:** Same week as initial contact. Brief delivered within 48 hours of call.

**Pricing model:** Free. This is a pure lead-generation tool. The brief is designed to be compelling enough to demonstrate expertise but insufficient for self-implementation — it surfaces problems that require the paid Diagnostic to properly scope.

**Strategic purpose:** Replaces traditional "free consultation" with a tangible deliverable that (a) builds trust, (b) creates a natural transition to the Diagnostic, and (c) provides a shareable asset the champion can circulate internally to build buy-in.

**Cost to deliver:** 2-3 hours of founder time per scan. At an internal opportunity cost of $200/hr, each free scan costs $400-600 in foregone billable time. This must be managed against a conversion target (see Upsell/Expansion Logic).

---

### Tier 1: AI Diagnostic (The Door-Opener)

**What it is:** A structured 2-4 week assessment of the client's operations, data infrastructure, and AI readiness. Produces a prioritized AI roadmap with business case estimates for each initiative.

**Deliverables:**
- Stakeholder interviews (4-8 interviews across departments)
- Current-state process mapping for 3-5 key workflows
- Data infrastructure audit (what data exists, where, quality, accessibility)
- AI readiness scorecard (team skills, infrastructure, governance, culture)
- Prioritized AI roadmap: 5-10 initiatives ranked by ROI, feasibility, and risk
- Executive presentation (30-45 min readout with leadership)
- Written report (15-30 pages depending on scope)

**Timeline:** 2-4 weeks depending on organization size and access to stakeholders.

**Pricing model:** Fixed project fee. Scoped during the Free Scan follow-up conversation.

**Why fixed-fee:** Diagnostics are repeatable and predictable in scope. Fixed fees reduce buyer friction ("what will this cost me?") and allow the founder to improve efficiency over time without reducing revenue.

---

### Tier 2: AI Brain Build (The Core Offering)

**What it is:** Design and implementation of agentic AI pipelines, workflows, and systems identified in the Diagnostic. This is the "brain" — not a one-off chatbot, but interconnected AI capabilities embedded into the client's operations.

**Deliverables:**
- Solution architecture document
- Working AI system(s): agentic pipelines, RAG systems, workflow automation, decision-support tools
- Integration with existing client systems (CRM, ERP, data warehouses, communication tools)
- Testing and validation (accuracy benchmarks, edge case handling)
- Documentation: technical architecture, user guides, runbooks
- Knowledge transfer: team training sessions (2-4 hours)
- 30-day post-launch support buffer

**Timeline:** 4-12 weeks depending on scope. Typical mid-market engagement is 6-8 weeks.

**Pricing model:** Project-based or value-based, depending on how clearly the ROI can be quantified.
- **Project-based:** Used when scope is well-defined but ROI is hard to measure (e.g., internal knowledge management system)
- **Value-based:** Used when the AI system has measurable financial impact (e.g., "this pipeline replaces 3 FTEs worth of manual processing" — price at 20-30% of first-year savings)

---

### Tier 3: AI Strategy & Adoption (The Wrapper)

**What it is:** Change management, team training, adoption roadmaps, and organizational alignment for AI transformation. Often bundled with or follows a Build engagement.

**Deliverables:**
- AI adoption roadmap (12-month plan for rolling out AI capabilities across the organization)
- Change management plan (communication strategy, champion identification, resistance management)
- Team training program (role-specific training on working with AI systems)
- AI governance framework (usage policies, data handling, quality standards, escalation procedures)
- Executive workshops (half-day or full-day sessions with leadership on AI strategy)
- Monthly adoption metrics dashboard and check-ins (for 2-3 months post-delivery)

**Timeline:** 2-6 weeks as a standalone; often overlaps with Build Phase.

**Pricing model:** Day-rate or project-based. Day-rates are common for workshop-style engagements. Project-based for full adoption programs.

---

### Tier 4: AI Brain Support (Recurring Revenue)

**What it is:** Ongoing monitoring, optimization, and iteration on deployed AI systems. Includes performance monitoring, model retraining/updating, prompt optimization, new feature additions, and strategic advisory.

**Deliverables (monthly):**
- System health monitoring and performance reports
- Prompt/model optimization based on production data
- Bug fixes and edge case resolution
- Monthly strategy call (30-60 min) reviewing AI performance and identifying expansion opportunities
- Priority response for production issues (4-hour SLA during business hours)
- Quarterly roadmap review and planning

**Optional add-ons:**
- New feature development (billed separately or included at higher tiers)
- Additional AI system builds (discounted for retainer clients)
- Team training refreshers

**Timeline:** Month-to-month with 30-day cancellation notice. Discounts for 6-month and 12-month commitments.

**Pricing model:** Monthly retainer with tiered options based on hours and scope.

#### Value-Based Pricing Benchmarks

Value-based pricing in AI consulting typically follows these models:

| Model | Typical Structure | When to Use |
|---|---|---|
| Percentage of savings | 15-30% of first-year documented savings | Process automation replacing manual labor |
| Percentage of revenue impact | 10-20% of attributable revenue increase | Revenue-generating AI (e.g., recommendation engines, lead scoring) |
| Per-outcome pricing | $X per successful outcome (e.g., per processed document) | High-volume, measurable AI tasks |
| Hybrid: base + performance | 50-70% as fixed base fee + 30-50% tied to KPIs | Complex projects where both parties share risk |

**Key insight for "AI Brain" pricing:** The sweet spot is a hybrid model for Build engagements — a fixed base fee that covers costs plus reasonable margin, with a performance bonus tied to measurable outcomes. This aligns incentives, reduces client risk perception, and captures upside when the AI system outperforms expectations.

### Margin Analysis Narrative

**Highest-margin services:**

1. **Enterprise AI Diagnostic (70%) and Enterprise Strategy (75%):** These are high-value, primarily knowledge-work engagements where the deliverable's value far exceeds the time investment. Enterprise clients pay a premium for structured frameworks and executive-ready presentations. Minimal hard costs.

2. **Enterprise AI Brain Build with contractors (65%):** Leveraging contractors at $75-125/hr while billing at enterprise rates creates healthy margins. The founder's time shifts from execution to architecture, review, and client management — higher-leverage activities.

3. **Mid-Market AI Strategy & Adoption (65%):** Similar to the Diagnostic — high knowledge leverage, low hard costs. The "wrapper" services are inherently high-margin because they're advisory-heavy.

**Lowest-margin services:**

1. **SMB AI Brain Build solo-delivered (8%):** This is a trap to avoid. Building a full AI system for $25,000 while investing 150 hours of founder time is barely above breakeven. Either use contractors to reduce founder hours, or increase prices to $35,000+ minimum.

2. **SMB AI Brain Support (31%):** Low retainer fees don't leave much room after the founder's time. Consider setting minimum retainer at $3,000/mo for SMBs or structuring as a lower-touch offering (5-8 hours/mo).

**Critical insight:** The solo founder should prioritize mid-market and enterprise Diagnostics and Strategy engagements for margin, and use contractors on Builds to maintain margin while freeing capacity. SMB Builds should only be taken with contractors or at premium pricing.

**Margin improvement levers:**
- **Templatize Diagnostics:** Develop reusable assessment frameworks, questionnaires, and report templates to reduce hours from 40 to 25-30 for mid-market — pushing margin from 59% to 70%+.
- **Componentize Builds:** Create reusable AI pipeline components (RAG system, document processor, workflow orchestrator) that reduce build time by 30-40% on repeat engagements.
- **Bundle Strategy with Build:** Selling Strategy as a 15-25% add-on to Build captures high-margin revenue with minimal incremental effort since the founder is already deeply embedded in the client's operations.
- **Tier Support offerings:** Offer "Lite" (5 hrs/mo), "Standard" (12 hrs/mo), and "Premium" (20+ hrs/mo) support tiers. Lite tier has the best margin for low-complexity deployed systems.

**Upgrade path:** ~20% of quick-hit clients return within 6-12 months for a second build or upgrade to a retainer once they see the first system's impact. Design the handoff to include a "future opportunities" section that plants seeds for expansion.

## Upsell/Expansion Logic

### The Service Flywheel

Each tier is designed to naturally lead to the next, creating a self-reinforcing cycle that increases customer lifetime value while reducing acquisition cost for subsequent engagements.

```
FREE AI OPPORTUNITY SCAN
  │
  │  "We found 3 major opportunities. Here's the impact.
  │   To properly scope and prioritize, you need a Diagnostic."
  │
  ▼  (Target conversion: 25-35%)
AI DIAGNOSTIC
  │
  │  "Your roadmap shows $X in potential savings/revenue.
  │   The #1 priority initiative has a 6-week build timeline."
  │
  ▼  (Target conversion: 50-65%)
AI BRAIN BUILD
  │
  │  "The system is live and performing. To maximize adoption
  │   and prepare your team, let's run a Strategy engagement."
  │
  ├──▶ AI STRATEGY & ADOPTION (often bundled with Build)
  │    (Target bundle rate: 40-60% of Build clients)
  │
  │  "Your AI systems need ongoing optimization and your
  │   team needs a strategic partner as you scale AI usage."
  │
  ▼  (Target conversion: 55-70%)
AI BRAIN SUPPORT (monthly retainer)
  │
  │  "Based on 3 months of production data, we see opportunities
  │   for a second AI system. Let's scope a Phase 2 Build."
  │
  ▼  (Target expansion: 30-40% of retainer clients)
SECOND BUILD → EXPANDED RETAINER → REFERRAL
```

### Transition Mechanics — How Each Service Sells the Next

#### Free Scan → Diagnostic

**The hook:** The Free Scan deliverable intentionally identifies opportunities but leaves them at the "compelling but not actionable" level. The brief says "you could save $500K/year by automating your document processing pipeline" but doesn't provide the architecture, data requirements, or implementation plan to do it.

**Transition language:** "Based on what I've seen, there are real, significant opportunities here. The next step is a structured Diagnostic that maps your data, interviews your team, and produces a prioritized roadmap with business cases. That's what turns these opportunities into an actionable plan."

**Conversion levers:**
- Include a rough ROI estimate in the Free Scan brief (creates urgency)
- Send a follow-up email 3 days after the scan with a relevant case study or article
- Offer to present findings to their broader team (expands the audience, builds internal champions)

#### Diagnostic → Build

**The hook:** The Diagnostic deliverable is a detailed roadmap with business cases for each initiative. It quantifies the opportunity (e.g., "$800K in annual savings from automating compliance review") and includes enough technical detail to be credible, but the client cannot self-implement.

**Transition language:** "Your roadmap is ready. Initiative #1 — [specific project] — has a projected ROI of [X] and can be built in [Y] weeks. I recommend we start there. Here's a proposal for the Build phase."

**Conversion levers:**
- Rank initiatives by "quick win" potential — the first Build should deliver visible results fast
- Include a "cost of delay" calculation (e.g., "every month you wait costs $67K in continued manual processing")
- Offer to present the roadmap to the executive team — this builds buy-in and positions you as the trusted advisor, making a competitive bid less likely

#### Build → Strategy & Adoption

**The hook:** During the Build, the founder observes firsthand how the team interacts with the new AI system, where resistance exists, and what organizational changes are needed. This creates a natural opening.

**Transition language:** "The system is performing well technically, but I'm seeing adoption gaps in [department/team]. Without a proper rollout plan and training, you'll only capture 40-50% of the potential value. Let me propose a Strategy & Adoption engagement to close that gap."

**Conversion levers:**
- Track and report adoption metrics during the 30-day post-launch support buffer
- Identify specific "adoption blockers" and present them alongside solutions
- Often best sold as a bundle with the Build at a 10-15% discount vs. standalone pricing

#### Build/Strategy → Support Retainer

**The hook:** AI systems are not "set and forget." Prompts degrade, models update, business requirements shift, and edge cases emerge in production. The founder has deep context from the Build that makes them uniquely efficient at ongoing optimization.

**Transition language:** "Your AI system is live and stable. But here's what I know from experience: the first 90 days of production will surface edge cases, optimization opportunities, and new feature requests that can 2-3x the system's impact. A Support retainer ensures we capture that upside and that the system keeps performing as your business evolves."

**Conversion levers:**
- During the 30-day post-launch buffer, document every production issue and improvement opportunity — this becomes the "here's what I've already done for you" pitch for ongoing support
- Offer a "launch special" — first 3 months at reduced rate with the option to cancel if not seeing value
- Frame the retainer as "AI team-as-a-service" — cheaper than hiring a full-time AI engineer ($150-250K/yr) but with the same ongoing attention

#### Support → Expansion (Second Build + Referral)

**The hook:** Monthly support calls naturally surface new opportunities. The founder, now deeply familiar with the client's operations, can spot high-impact AI applications in adjacent departments or workflows.

**Transition language:** "In our last three monthly reviews, we've identified opportunities in [department/workflow] that could deliver [estimated impact]. I'd recommend we scope a Phase 2 Build to extend the AI Brain into those areas."

**Conversion levers:**
- Include "expansion opportunity radar" as a standard section in monthly support reports
- Offer preferred pricing for retainer clients (10-15% discount on additional Builds)
- Ask for referral introductions once the client has seen 3+ months of results — timing matters; too early feels premature, too late misses the enthusiasm window

### The Flywheel Effect on Unit Economics

As the founder builds a base of retainer clients, the business economics improve dramatically:

| Metric | Month 6 (1 retainer) | Month 12 (3 retainers) | Month 18 (5 retainers) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly Recurring Revenue | $6,000 | $18,000 | $30,000 |
| Annual run-rate from retainers alone | $72,000 | $216,000 | $360,000 |
| Hours committed to support/mo | 15 hrs | 45 hrs | 75 hrs |
| Remaining capacity for new Builds | 145 hrs/mo | 115 hrs/mo | 85 hrs/mo |
| Retainer revenue as % of break-even | 30% | 90% | 150% |

**Key insight:** By Month 18, retainer revenue alone can exceed the founder's break-even point ($20,000-25,000/mo including personal draw). This means all project revenue (Diagnostics, Builds) becomes pure growth — fundable into contractors, marketing, or personal income increases.
