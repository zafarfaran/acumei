# Acumei Fiverr Gig Catalog Design

## Objective

Create a complete, search-focused Fiverr catalog for Acumei covering AI application development, machine learning, Python development, and related web development. The catalog will contain 15 fully drafted gigs, with four selected for immediate publication under Fiverr's new-freelancer limit and the remaining 11 held as a launch queue.

The catalog must attract both technical buyers seeking implementation help and small-business buyers seeking practical outcomes. It must position Acumei as an accessible entry point with premium engineering depth, not as a low-cost commodity provider.

## Platform Constraints

Current Fiverr guidance establishes the following constraints:

- A new freelancer may have up to four active gigs.
- Level 1 and Level 2 freelancers may have up to ten active gigs.
- Top Rated freelancers may have up to 30 active gigs.
- Gig titles use Fiverr's prefilled "I will" format and must remain within the live title limit.
- Each gig may use up to five search tags.
- Up to three packages may be offered: Basic, Standard, and Premium.
- Gig descriptions have a 1,200-character limit.
- Fiverr supports up to ten FAQs.
- At least one original gallery image is required; up to three images may be used.
- Category selection must be checked carefully before publication because a published gig's category cannot be changed.

The deliverable will recommend categories, subcategories, service types, and metadata based on current Fiverr options. The publisher must verify those selections in the live editor before publishing because Fiverr may change its taxonomy.

## Catalog Strategy

The catalog will use a search-intent funnel. Each gig will target one distinct primary buyer query and one bounded service rather than combining every capability into broad, overlapping listings.

The catalog has three service pillars:

1. AI applications: RAG, AI SaaS, LLM integrations, and agents.
2. Machine learning: prediction, computer vision, NLP, forecasting, recommendations, and deployment.
3. Python and web development: automation, APIs, Django/Flask applications, scraping, debugging, and testing.

This structure creates multiple independent search entry points while allowing relevant gigs to cross-sell larger implementations.

## Required Content for Every Gig

Each gig draft will contain:

- Fiverr-ready title centred on one primary search query.
- Primary keyword, secondary keywords, and buyer intent.
- Recommended category, subcategory, service type, and relevant metadata.
- Five relevant search tags.
- Basic, Standard, and Premium package names.
- Price, scope, deliverables, delivery time, and revision count for each package.
- Clear boundaries between packages.
- Optional extras and rush-delivery pricing.
- A complete description within Fiverr's 1,200-character limit.
- Five service-specific FAQs.
- Buyer requirement questions marked required or optional.
- Three gallery-image concepts with exact headline and subheadline copy.
- A short gig-video script.
- Relevant portfolio proof.
- Scope exclusions and assumptions.
- Source-code and ownership terms.
- Third-party API, cloud, model, data, and hosting cost notes.
- A contact-before-ordering rule for complex or uncertain work.
- Relevant cross-sells to other Acumei gigs.

All copy must be ready to paste into Fiverr. Character-sensitive fields must be checked during production.

## Pricing Design

Acumei will use accessible starter packages with premium upgrades. Basic packages will be genuinely useful but tightly bounded diagnostics, prototypes, scripts, or small features. They will not promise a production system at an unsustainable price.

Initial pricing anchors for the launch gigs are:

- Python automation: $75 / $225 / $600.
- Machine-learning prediction model: $125 / $400 / $950.
- RAG chatbot web app: $175 / $550 / $1,500.
- AI SaaS MVP: $300 / $900 / $2,500.

Package scope, not only delivery speed, must increase between tiers. Production deployment, authentication, persistent storage, observability, complex integrations, and polished frontends belong in higher tiers or custom offers.

The queued gigs will follow the same pricing principle, with prices adjusted to delivery effort and risk. Unclear, security-sensitive, regulated, or integration-heavy projects require a pre-order conversation and may require a custom offer.

## Search Optimisation Design

Every gig will focus on one primary query that reflects buyer language. The primary phrase will appear naturally in the title, opening description, and package language. Supporting keywords will appear in tags, FAQs, and body copy where relevant.

The catalog will:

- Use all five available tags.
- Prefer specific buyer phrases over vague terms such as "AI expert."
- Keep different gigs focused on different primary searches.
- Use readable copy rather than keyword stuffing.
- Match category and metadata to the actual service.
- Lead thumbnails with the service and outcome, not a list of technologies.
- Use proof and scope clarity to improve click-to-message and message-to-order conversion.

No listing will promise a top search position. Fiverr visibility also depends on click-through rate, conversion, reviews, responsiveness, delivery performance, client satisfaction, and competition.

## Gallery and Video Direction

Gallery images will use clean, high-contrast layouts suitable for small Fiverr search cards. Each gig will have:

1. A primary card stating the service and buyer outcome.
2. A scope card showing the principal deliverables or workflow.
3. A proof card featuring the most relevant project, architecture, or verified result.

Relevant screenshots may be drawn from Ovalens and other public portfolio projects. Screenshots must not expose private data, employer systems, credentials, or confidential interfaces.

Video scripts will be short and buyer-focused:

1. State the buyer's problem.
2. Explain the bounded service.
3. Show one relevant proof point.
4. Clarify what the buyer receives.
5. Ask buyers with complex requirements to message before ordering.

## Scope and Risk Controls

Every gig must define what is not included. Common exclusions include paid API usage, cloud hosting, commercial data licences, ongoing maintenance, complex UI design, unrestricted revisions, data labelling, and third-party account fees unless explicitly included.

The catalog will not:

- Invent clients, results, testimonials, certifications, or product metrics.
- Guarantee AI accuracy, revenue, savings, search ranking, or regulatory compliance.
- Describe a prototype as production-ready.
- Offer academic cheating or deceptive automation.
- Offer scraping that bypasses access controls or violates applicable terms and law.
- Claim employer work as an Acumei engagement.

Regulated financial, medical, legal, or employment use cases will include appropriate human-review and non-advice language.

## Publication and Optimisation

1. Complete the Fiverr company profile and portfolio entries before or alongside launch.
2. Publish the four launch gigs permitted for a new freelancer.
3. Keep titles and categories stable during the first 30-day measurement period unless a factual or policy error requires correction.
4. Track impressions, clicks, messages, orders, and conversion separately for each gig.
5. If a gig receives impressions but few clicks, test its primary gallery card and opening promise.
6. If a gig receives clicks but few messages, improve proof, scope clarity, packages, and FAQs.
7. If it receives messages but few orders, inspect qualification, response time, pricing, and package fit.
8. Prepare all queued listings in advance, then activate the strongest additional six when the account reaches Level 1.
9. Rotate weak gigs only after sufficient evidence; do not churn keywords weekly.

## Success Criteria

The catalog is complete when:

- All 15 gigs contain every required field and asset brief.
- The first four listings fit Fiverr's new-freelancer active-gig limit.
- No two gigs target the same primary query or offer materially duplicate scopes.
- All titles and descriptions comply with live Fiverr field limits.
- Every metric and experience claim maps to public portfolio or CV evidence.
- Package boundaries prevent accidental full-build commitments at starter prices.
- Each gig has a clear buyer, outcome, proof point, and next-step cross-sell.
- The content can be pasted into Fiverr with only live category selection and image production remaining.
